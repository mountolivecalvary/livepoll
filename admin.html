⚠️ Why are changes not saving to Supabase?
On Supabase's free tier, projects pause automatically after 7 days of inactivity. If your project paused, simply log into supabase.com/dashboard and click "Restore project".

⚡ Cloud Sync Live: Even when Supabase is offline or paused, our built-in Cloud WebSocket Sync actively keeps all audience phones, projectors, and remotes in real-time sync across the internet!
SQL Schema: A complete database schema file is included in your project as supabase_schema.sql. Paste it into your Supabase SQL Editor to initialize all tables and realtime replication.
